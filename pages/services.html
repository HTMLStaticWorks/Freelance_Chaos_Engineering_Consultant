Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about chaos engineering.

Is it safe to run experiments in production?

We always start in staging. However, true resilience can only be verified in production. We use strict blast radius controls and automated rollbacks to ensure safety.

How long does a typical engagement last?

Audits take 1-2 weeks. Game Day facilitation can be a one-off or a recurring monthly/quarterly engagement.

What tools do you use?

We are tool-agnostic but have deep expertise in LitmusChaos, Gremlin, AWS Fault Injection Simulator, and Chaos Mesh.
