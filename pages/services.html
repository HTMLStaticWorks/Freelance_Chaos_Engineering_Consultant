The Chaos Framework

How I Harden Systems

A systematic, scientific approach to identifying vulnerabilities before they become outages.

01. Steady State

I define your system's normal behavior through metrics like latency, error rates, and throughput.

02. Hypothesis

I formulate what will happen when I inject a fault (e.g., "If node A fails, traffic redirects to node B").

03. Blast Radius

Controlled execution of experiments with automatic rollback if SLOs are violated.
