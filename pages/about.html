My Working Approach

A disciplined, scientific methodology ensuring safety and maximum learning value for your infrastructure.

01

Discovery & SLOs

I analyze system architecture, identify critical customer paths, and map current steady-state metrics and alert systems.

02

I draft scenarios (e.g. database network loss) and document expected behaviors under controlled conditions, defining a strict blast radius.
