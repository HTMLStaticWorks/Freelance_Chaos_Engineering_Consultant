We are a team of distributed systems experts dedicated to making software immune to failure through the science of chaos engineering.

Our Mission: Democratizing Chaos

Resilience Labs was founded with a simple observation: modern systems are too complex to be understood by intuition alone. We need data-driven evidence of how our systems behave under stress.

Our mission is to democratize chaos engineering, making it accessible not just to big tech, but to every organization that values its uptime and customer trust.
