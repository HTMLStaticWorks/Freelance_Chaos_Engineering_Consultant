Our Mission: Democratizing Chaos

Chaos was founded with a simple observation: modern systems are too complex to be understood by intuition alone. We need data-driven evidence of how our systems behave under stress.

Our mission is to democratize chaos engineering, making it accessible not just to big tech, but to every organization that values its uptime and customer trust.

The Chaos Manifesto

The core principles that guide every architectural resilience experiment we design.

Safety First

We prioritize blast radius control to ensure experiments never impact real customers or downstream services.

Scientific Rigor

If we can't measure it, we don't break it. Every test starts with a steady-state metric and a clear hypothesis.

Continuous Validation

Chaos engineering is not a one-time event; it's a culture of continuous verification in production.

Automation Driven

Manually testing is slow. We build automated resilience gates directly into your CI/CD pipelines.

Shared Ownership

Reliability is everyone's responsibility. We bridge the gap between Dev, Ops, and Product teams.

Blast Radius Control

We use surgical fault injection to isolate impact and ensure zero disruption to end-user experiences.
